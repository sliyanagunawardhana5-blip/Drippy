Install App to Phone

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen' .
  3. Tap 'Add' in the top right corner.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the 3 Dots menu top right.
  2. Tap 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.
  3. Follow screen prompts to confirm install.

Notification Guide

1. Disable 'Do Not Disturb'

DND or Focus Modes suppress lockscreen banners and sound alarms on iOS/Android.

2. Allow Browser Notifications

Go to Phone Settings -> Apps -> Chrome / Safari / Drippy -> Notifications -> Allow All.

3. Keep Tab/App in Background

PWA background worker will keep tracking countdowns when installed to Home Screen.
